## Brand & Style

This design system expresses heavy industrial engineering through the restraint, discipline, and architectural clarity of high-end Swiss editorial publication. It discards common industrial tropes—such as weathered metal textures, safety chevrons, and uncalibrated caution colors—in favor of structural authority, tectonic precision, and monumental scale.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
- **Audience:** Civil engineers, infrastructure project leads, heavy logistics directors, and architectural contractors commissioning telescopic crane operations.
- **Impression:** Absolute mechanical reliability, calculating safety, bespoke engineering pedigree, and commanding presence.
- **Design Philosophy:** Structural Modernism meets Editorial Brutalism. Information is treated like architectural blueprints: framed by rigid technical gridlines, high-contrast display typography, precise fractional metrics, and generous, intentional whitespace that gives weight to the machinery.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from hydraulic crane architecture, raw structural steel, and archival architectural blueprints. The interface operates fundamentally in dark mode to mirror heavy machinery cabins, structural drafting rooms, and nighttime infrastructure engineering.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#E3A12B` - Crane Amber):** A disciplined, rich machinery gold. Used deliberately for focal highlights, operational states, active telemetry indicators, and structural emphasis. Never overused as a background wash.
- **Secondary (`#737B84` - Steel Gray):** The color of machined counterweights, hydraulic cylinders, and technical annotation. Provides subtle hierarchy in subheaders, structural metadata, and muted outlines.
- **Tertiary (`#F1F0EC` - Paper / Warm Off-White):** Used primarily for editorial high-contrast display text, monumental load numbers, and crisp white-space zones on print-grade collateral or inverted specs panels.
- **Neutral (`#171C22` - Industrial Black):** The default root canvas, conveying dense ballast and physical presence without relying on pitch black.
- **Neutral Variant (`#0F1317` - Deep Charcoal):** Background for receded zones, recessed container bays, technical sidebars, and grid substrates.
- **Surface White (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved strictly for razor-sharp hairline dividers, illuminated instrument indicators, and pure visual anchor points.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances monumentality with technical calibration.

- **Headlines & Metrics (Space Grotesk):** Engineered geometry with subtle mechanical incisions. Large-format tonnage numbers and boom length metrics use `metric-monumental` to command physical scale on screen.
- **Body & Continuous Reading (Hanken Grotesk):** Contemporary, hyper-clean grotesque with balanced legibility across dense engineering logs, service descriptions, and architectural case studies.
- **Technical Readouts & Micro-Labels (JetBrains Mono):** Always rendered in full uppercase with expansive tracking (`0.18em`) for section coordinates, machine IDs, crane capacity charts, and operational telemetry (e.g., `CAP. 200T // BOOM 72M // LOC. ANKARA`).

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a strict, visible structural grid derived from Swiss poster design and structural assembly blueprints. 

### Grid Philosophy
- **Grid Structure:** A 12-column architectural grid on desktop (`1200px+`) transitioning to 6-column on tablet (`768px - 1199px`) and 2-column or 4-column on mobile (`< 768px`).
- **Visible Gridlines (Tectonic Framing):** Columns and rows are defined by continuous 1px or 0.5px hairline rules in `#737B84` at 20% opacity. Structural sections are bounded by explicit mechanical frames rather than invisible margins.
- **Asymmetric Anchoring:** High-density technical metadata is pinned to grid margins, while generous architectural open space surrounds machinery imagery and crane elevation diagrams.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids generic drop shadows, diffused lighting, and artificial dimensional depth. Depth is created exclusively through planar tension, layering, and line-weight hierarchy.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Root Datum (`#171C22`):** Primary structural bedrock canvas.
2. **Sub-Grade / Cavity (`#0F1317`):** Recessed containers, technical specs sheets, and code-block readouts; positioned flush or framed within grid panels.
3. **Overlaid Plates (`#171C22` with 1px `#737B84` hairline outline):** Floating specs cards, technical overlays, and modal views. Rather than casting a shadow, overlays feature a 1px solid border (`rgba(227, 161, 43, 0.4)` on interactive focus) and a sharp 0.5px interior inset border.
4. **Z-Index Layering:** Technical diagrams, crane boom schematics, and high-contrast typographic numerals layer physically beneath or behind masked photography of the telescopic booms, creating authentic cinematic depth.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly sharp (`0px` border-radius). 

### Geometry Rules
- Every component—buttons, input fields, image viewports, status chips, and modal sheets—is cut to razor-sharp `0px` corners.
- Physical engineering components are machined with zero border radius; the software reflects this mechanical truth.
- Chamfered corners (45-degree corner notches of 4px to 8px) may be deployed strictly for technical badges, vehicle fleet identifiers, and load indicator modules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#E3A12B` background with `#0F1317` bold typography (`Space Grotesk`, medium/bold, uppercase, `letter-spacing: 0.1em`). 0px radius. Hover state transitions to inverse `#F1F0EC` fill with pure `#171C22` text.
- **Secondary / Technical:** Transparent background with 1px solid `#737B84` border. Text in `#F1F0EC` (`JetBrains Mono`, uppercase). Hover triggers 1px solid `#E3A12B` with `#E3A12B` text.
- **Action Icons:** Encased in square 1:1 ratio boxes framed by 1px hairline borders.

### Telemetry Chips & Spec Tags
- Monospaced, zero-radius micro-containers with 1px border (`#737B84` at 30% opacity).
- Prefix indicators: A 4px × 4px square dot in `#E3A12B` for active status, or `#737B84` for standby.
- Format: `[ TONNAGE: 120T ]`, `[ REACH: 64M ]`, `[ STATUS: DEPLOYED ]`.

### Lists & Equipment Spec Tables
- Tabular architectural layout with persistent 0.5px horizontal rules separating entries.
- Column headers formatted in `label-caps` (`#737B84`).
- Data values set in `code-spec` or `Space Grotesk` tabular numbers for rapid optical scanning.
- Interactive hover fills the entire row with `#0F1317` and shifts the left accent indicator to Crane Amber (`#E3A12B`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkbox:** Square box (16px × 16px), 1px solid `#737B84`, sharp corners. Checked state: solid `#E3A12B` fill with an inset black technical square cross or dot.
- **Radio Button:** Square diamond or solid inner square indicator instead of circular geometry to preserve the mechanical aesthetic.

### Input Fields
- Underlined or four-sided hairline framed boxes with `#0F1317` surface fill.
- Inactive border: 1px `#737B84` (35% opacity).
- Active/Focus: 1px `#E3A12B` with accompanying monospaced input coordinate label shifting from `#737B84` to `#E3A12B`.
- Placeholder text rendered in muted monospaced text with coordinate prefixes (e.g., `LOC // ENTER SITE ADDRESS`).

### Cards & Fleet Showcase Modules
- Heavy machinery portfolio items framed by a 1px perimeter line.
- Card footers feature technical specification strips: boom length, counterweight tonnage, and outrigger span formatted in grid-locked monospaced cells.
- Imagery treatment: High-contrast, authentic photography of mobile telescopic cranes against Ankara skylines or industrial construction sites, desaturated by 15% with high tonal blacks to harmonize with `#171C22`.

### Bespoke Domain Components
- **Boom Telemetry Visualizer:** An interactive coordinate matrix illustrating telescopic boom angles, load radius, and rated capacity charts.
- **Job Safety Certificate Stamp:** A high-contrast, framed typographic lockup verifying EN 13000 safety standards and Turkish Chamber of Mechanical Engineers compliance.
